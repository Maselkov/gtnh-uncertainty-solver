Button presses

    Before you press anything

    • If a cell has a yellow box around it, one button is already selected. Click that same button once to clear it.
    • Each step is two clicks: the yellow button, then the blue one. The two cells swap.
    • The button grid matches the matrix: the left two columns are matrix columns 1–2, the right two are columns 3–4.

    How it is checked

    Each active LED weighs a group of cells. Cells mirrored through the LED form pairs, and the LED is green when the summed pair differences stay below 128 × the group size (out of 0–999 per cell). The solver searches every 1-, 2- and 3-swap sequence for the one with the most headroom, and plans with a safety margin of 2 points per pair to absorb rounding in the screenshot.

    Screenshot tips

    After opening the GUI, wait until the LEDs appear before taking the screenshot; they can take a few seconds, and until then the cells may show placeholder values. Use PNG (F2 in Minecraft) and any GUI scale. The value-assist numbers can be on or off; cells are read from their blue level. All cells slowly fade together over time, which does not affect balance.
